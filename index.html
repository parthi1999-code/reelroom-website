About us

We don't just build apartments — we build homes, communities and the trust that comes with them.

Founded in 2014 as Sanvi Group and renamed Prashi Group in 2024, we're a family of builders who've spent a decade delivering Vastu-compliant, RERA-approved homes across Bengaluru.
