Our legacy

A decade of excellence

Prashi Group, formerly Sanvi Group, was founded in 2014 by a family of builders who saw the potential in Bengaluru's fast-changing real estate landscape.

Under the Sanvi name, our team completed a series of premium residential projects, each one built on quality construction, thoughtful design and customer satisfaction.

In 2024 we became Prashi Group. It wasn't just a new name. It brought a decade of experience together with new capabilities and a clear vision for the homebuyers of tomorrow, under the same leadership that guided Sanvi Group.

Our journey
